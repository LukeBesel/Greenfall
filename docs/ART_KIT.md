# Art kit (art/kit/**)

Shared building and prop kit for every planet. Owner's bar: **Moon Sweepers hub quality**:
chunky, bevelled, greebled, colourful and well lit. Owner: helper `kit`. Other helpers use
the kit and don't edit it; ask kit (via main) for changes.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `art/kit/kit_materials.gd` (`KitMat`) | Cached materials: painted metal, steel, rusty steel, concrete, hazard, rubber, glass, emissive trims, leaves, dirt, decals. States: broken / repaired / restored. |
| `art/kit/kit_surface.gdshader` | One PBR shader for paint, steel, rust, concrete and hazard. Triplanar Poly Haven textures with no UVs needed, paint chipped at baked edges, grime from the ground up, dust on top faces. |
| `art/kit/kit_trim.gdshader` | Emissive trims. Glow colour comes from each vertex, with a `powered` switch. |
| `art/kit/kit_mesh.gd` (`KitMesh`) | `Builder` (merges parts into one mesh per material), primitives (bevelled box, bevelled cylinder, lathe, tube sweep, quad, decal quad, pebble, grass tuft), `panel_wall`, `ground_patch`, caching. |
| `art/kit/kit_props.gd` (`KitProps`) | Pipe runs with elbows and flanges, cable sag, vents, railings, ladders, stairs, antenna, dish, tank, solar panel, hatch/door, lamp post, crate/crate stack, barrel, planter, bush, debris, wind turbine (`wind_turbine` tower plus a spinning `turbine_rotor` placed at `turbine_hub()`). |
| `art/kit/kit_buildings.gd` (`KitBuilding`) | Ready-made buildings with `set_state` / `set_powered`: `module_small`, `module_medium`, `module_large`, `tower`, `hangar`, `tank_farm`, `machine_housing`, `dome`, `greenhouse` (the dome and greenhouse fill with plants when restored). |
| `art/kit/decals/` | Decal images plus `atlas.png`/`atlas.json`. Generated by `tools/kit_decals.py`. |
| `art/kit/kit_lineup.tscn` | Lineup and render rig. Every piece in all three states, day and night. |

## Quick start

```gdscript
# A whole ready-made building: ground patch, clutter and lights included.
var hub := KitBuilding.create("module_medium", "broken", 3, {"main": "cream", "accent": "teal"})
add_child(hub)
hub.set_state("repaired")    # when the player fixes it (rebuilds from cache)
hub.set_powered(true)        # trims, lit windows and lamps; never lit while broken

# Your own model from kit parts (one MeshInstance3D, about 8-12 draw calls in total):
var b := KitMesh.Builder.new(seed, {"main": "orange", "accent": "navy"}, state)
b.box("paint:main", Vector3(2, 1.2, 1.4), KitMesh.xf(Vector3(0, 0.6, 0)))   # bevel picked automatically
b.cyl("paint:accent", 0.4, 1.0, KitMesh.xf(Vector3(1.2, 0, 0)))            # bevelled cylinder on y = 0
b.rod("steel", Vector3(0, 0, 0), Vector3(0, 2, 0), 0.04)                  # strut between two points
KitMesh.panel_wall(b, 4.0, 2.5, KitMesh.xf(Vector3(-2, 0, -1)), {"missing": 0.2 if state == "broken" else 0.0})
KitProps.pipe_run(b, Transform3D.IDENTITY, PackedVector3Array([Vector3(0, 0.5, 1), Vector3(0, 2, 1), Vector3(2, 2, 1)]))
KitProps.lamp_post(b, KitMesh.xf(Vector3(3, 0, 2)), 4.2, state)
b.decal("label_hub", Vector2(1.4, 0.35), KitMesh.xf(Vector3(0, 1.0, 0.71)))  # an image, never 3D text
var mi := KitMesh.instance(b)               # commits and applies materials for b.state
add_child(mi)
add_child(KitMesh.ground_patch(Vector2(8, 6), state, seed))   # instead of a flat slab
# b.lights holds the point lights the props asked for ({pos, color, range, energy}).
# Create OmniLight3D nodes for them when the building is powered (KitBuilding does this for you).
```

Cache what you build: `KitMesh.cached("mything:%s:%d" % [state, seed], func() -> Resource: ...)`.
Primitives are already cached by their parameters.

## Material keys

Every part is added under a key. The Builder routes it to a surface, and `KitMat.apply`
assigns that surface's material for the state:

| Key | Surface | Look |
|---|---|---|
| `paint:<role>` | `paint` | Painted metal. Role resolves through the palette (`main`, `accent`, `pipe`, `rail`, `pole`, `crate`, `barrel`, `patch`, `flower_a`, `flower_b`) or is a colour name or `#rrggbb`. The colour is baked per vertex, so **all paint is one draw call**. |
| `hazard` | `paint` | Yellow/black stripes (palette `hazard` sets the yellow). |
| `steel` / `dark_steel` | own | Bare brushed plates. Dark steel is for frames, backings and greebles. |
| `rusty` | own | Rusted steel (debris, boards, broken bits). |
| `concrete` | own | Plinths, kerbs, plates. |
| `rubber` | own | Cables, tyres, hoses. |
| `glass` | own | Windows. Warm interior glow when powered and not broken. |
| `glasshouse` | own | Light see-through greenhouse/dome panes (alpha). Grimy when broken. |
| `trim:<warm|cool|green|red|white>` | `trim` | Thin emissive strips and lenses. All trims share one draw call. They're dark when unpowered or broken. |
| `leaves` | own | Bushes in planters (dry when broken). |
| `dirt` | own | Soil in planters and ground skirts. |
| `decal` (via `b.decal(name, ...)`) | `decal` | Atlas decals: `arrow`, `chevrons`, `warn`, `power`, `logo`, `num_01`..`num_06`, `label_hub/power/water/dock/scrap/seeds`, `solar_cells`, `grille`. |

**Palettes.** Pass roles to `Builder.new(seed, palette, state)` or `KitBuilding.create(...)`.
`broken_<role>` overrides a role in the broken state. `trim_<kind>` remaps a glow kind, for
example `{"trim_cool": "green"}`. Per-planet suggestions:
- Verdance: cream / teal.
- Anvil: `#d9c7a8` / orange, with `trim_cool: warm`.
- Ochre: white / red.
- Tidewell: white / sky.
- Rime: `#e8eef2` / navy.

## States

| State | Look |
|---|---|
| `broken` | Heavy rust, chipped and faded paint, grime, missing or tilted panels, boarded windows, toppled crates and barrels, leaning antennas, fallen dishes, debris, cracked and missing ground plates, dry grass. **Nothing glows.** |
| `repaired` | Patched: some mismatched primer-grey panels, moderate edge wear, lights on. |
| `restored` | Fresh paint with a light clear coat, edge wear only, planters with bushes and flowers, painted floor lines and arrows, more grass at the edges. |

## Baked vertex data (for anyone writing their own shader on kit meshes)

- `COLOR.r`: edge mask. 1 on bevels, fading to 0 about 6 cm onto flat faces.
- `COLOR.g`: AO. Darker at the bottom of every part, which gives cheap contact shadows.
- `COLOR.b`: per-part random seed.
- `CUSTOM0`: paint or glow colour. `a = 1` means hazard stripes (on paint) or emissive (on trim).

## The approved look (owner, item 74)

The owner looked at the v6 yard machines and landmarks and said "Renders look great", then:
"Keep that same style and quality for anything else we build." So those are the reference for
every new machine, landmark, prop and planet:

- Reference shots (render them with `res://tools/yard_lineup.tscn -- --shots=v6 --out=screenshots/yard/v6`):
  picture signs, auto-baler, belt drive, recycler, and the landmarks (water tower, windpump,
  gantry crane, signal mast, big wheel, tall stack, tower crane, last bus, lighthouse).
- Models: `systems/camp/machine_art.gd`, `planet/sites/hub_landmarks.gd`; colours and sizes in
  `data/machine_art.json`, `data/landmarks.json`.
- What makes the look: chunky friendly shapes, the same paint palette, hazard stripes, picture
  signs with no words, a small console or lamp that glows when it works, and a real interior
  wherever you can see inside (the pod's old interior was the one thing the owner rejected).
- Before handing off new art: render it, look at it, and compare it with the reference shots.

## Art rules (owner's bar, from owners.md)

- Chunky, readable silhouettes. Every edge bevelled: use `b.box` / `b.cyl`, never a raw `BoxMesh` or `CylinderMesh`.
- Layered detail: panels with real seams, bolts, trims, vents, cables, pipes, hazard stripes, and decals as images.
- Small warm/cool emissive accents, and only where powered. Thin glow, "futuristic but not tron".
- Rich but cohesive colour: one main and one accent per building, with orange/yellow/red only on utility parts (pipes, rails, barrels).
- Ground every prop: the shader grimes from y = 0, and `ground_patch` adds kerbs, cracks, grit and tufts. Never put a flat colour slab under a site.
- Put lived-in clutter around each building (barrels, crates, lamp post, planters or debris).
- Broken versus restored must read at a glance, and the broken state has no glow at all.
- Rejected by the owner (don't repeat): plain white CG turbines, flat brown slab aprons, flat green carpets, streaky noise textures, empty plains, thin sticks.

## Performance

Measured on Nov 8, seed 1, with `KitBuilding.mesh_for` plus its ground patch. "Draw calls" counts
mesh surfaces. Each one is drawn once more in the shadow pass when it casts shadows. Grit and tufts don't cast shadows.

| Kind | Body draw calls | Patch draw calls | MultiMesh | Triangles (LOD0) | Lights (powered only) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| module_small | 9 | 3-4 | 2 | 32-35k | 1-2 |
| module_medium | 9-10 | 3-4 | 2 | 62-69k | 1-2 |
| module_large | 9 | 3-5 | 2 | 74-81k | 1-2 |
| tower | 9 | 3-4 | 2 | 24k | 1-2 |
| hangar | 7-8 | 3-5 | 2 | 27-28k | 1-2 |
| tank_farm | 8 | 3-5 | 2 | 27k | 1-2 |
| machine_housing | 8-9 | 3-4 | 2 | 21-22k | 0-1 |
| dome | 9 | 3-5 | 2 | 19k | 2-3 |
| greenhouse | 10-11 | 3-4 | 2 | 24-27k | 2-3 |

- **A building costs about 14 draw calls** in total (body, patch and MultiMeshes), whatever its detail. A site with 6 buildings is about 85 calls, or about 170 with shadows. That's fine on a Mac.
- All paint colours and hazard stripes share one `paint` surface, because the colour is stored per vertex. All trims share one `trim` surface, and all decals share one atlas surface.
- Bodies get automatic LODs (ImporterMesh, 3-5 levels), so distant buildings draw a few thousand triangles. LOD0 is heavy only up close.
- Lights are shadowless OmniLights, 1-3 per building, and they're on only when powered and not broken.
- The first build takes 100-300 ms per kind and state, including LOD generation; after that it's a cache hit. Preload the kinds a site uses during loading: `KitBuilding.mesh_for(kind, state, seed, palette)`.
- The glasshouse panes (greenhouse, dome) are the kit's only alpha-blended surface. Keep it to one or two glass buildings in view.
- For many identical props, build the mesh once (`KitMesh.cached`) and draw it with a MultiMesh.

## Tools

- Tests: `tests/test_art_kit.gd` (part of `tools/run_tests.sh`).
- Lineup render (gated to one render at a time, 960x540): `tools/kit_lineup.sh screenshots/kit/lineup [--only=tower,hangar] [--day-only|--night-only]`.
  You can also open `res://art/kit/kit_lineup.tscn` directly, or run the game with `--kit-lineup`.
- Contact sheet of the shots: `python3 tools/kit_sheet.py screenshots/kit/lineup`.
- Decals: edit `tools/kit_decals.py` and re-run it to rebuild the PNGs and the atlas.
