# Future notes

Short notes only. Nothing here is designed or scheduled. Planet 1 is the only planet being finished now.

## Parked content (built earlier; revisit after Planet 1 ships)
- Four later worlds with their own environments: a desert world, an ocean world, an ice world and a volcanic world.
- A solar-system travel layer and rocket tiers.
- A post-game prestige mode with carried bonuses, and an endless seed-delivery loop.
- A robot-building station for very large crews.
- Weekly seed challenge, museum, three save profiles.

## Ideas for later planets
- Each planet should bring one pollution family Planet 1 does not have (for example: drifting sand that re-buries cleared ground, tides that move floating waste, ice that locks waste until thawed).
- Returning to an earlier planet with a later tool could open optional extra ground. It must never be required for a planet's own ending.
- Touch controls and a mobile layout.
- Higher robot tiers and larger crews; bigger currency numbers as planets get harder.

- **Visible robot transport between cities** (M3-20): robots are shipped unseen today; a crawler or the rocket hold could carry them on screen.

- **Fifteen loose look pieces without a find yet** (M3-14): round, heart and bar eyes, antenna, halo, heart plate, med kit, watering can, tiny drone, and Graphite, Slate, Chalk, Orange, Gold and Bloom green paints. Place them in later planets' finds tables.

- **Native review of all translations** (de, es, fr, ja, pt_BR, zh_CN): every string is a machine draft. The CJK font lacks some glyphs (two zh_CN strings were reworded around 坩埚穹); add a fuller CJK font before release.
