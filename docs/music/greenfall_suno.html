Greenfall Suno Prompts

91 instrumental songs: three story themes, then a playlist for each planet. Bops, chill songs to get lost in, feel-good and lighthearted tracks, emotional pieces, and spooky night music. Press Copy and paste into Suno. Some songs also have a shorter Version B to try.

In Suno: Create, Custom mode, Instrumental on. Paste the prompt into Style of Music and type the song name into Title. Leave Lyrics empty.

Simple mode also works: paste into the song description. If Suno says it is too long, use Custom mode.

Mark made keeps track of finished songs in this browser.
