The story

A seed-keeper, a long way from home

  1. 01 · The storm

    Blown off the ark

    Centuries after Verdance drowned in its own waste, the ark Cradle carries the last seed vault through the dark. A meteor storm tears the hull, and a cargo pod spins loose with Lumi inside, still hugging a tray of seedlings.

  2. 02 · The fall

    No green left

    He falls for days and lands on a world of rubbish. The ark can't turn back; WREN, its AI, can only whisper over a failing relay. Lumi's core runs on green light, and there isn't a leaf in sight.

  3. 03 · The bloom

    Grow your way home

    Every plant he grows charges his Bloom Core. Clean the ground, raise a crew, bring the old bases back to life and rebuild the launch sites until a rocket can reach the ark.

What you do

From one tractor beam to a whole green planet

It starts with a single robot and a hopper. It ends with crews, cities and rockets, and every step makes the world visibly brighter.

  • Clean & bale

    Beam junk into your hopper, press it into tidy bales and sell them at the scale. A perfectly packed cube just feels right.

  • Build a robot crew

    Scoopies, Haul Bugs, Seedlings, Drips and Skimmers. Build them at the workbench and upgrade them from rusty Mk I to sleek Mk IV.

  • Command view

    Lift the camera over the real district, mark hexes and send each crew where it's needed: priority, stay, follow me or recall.

  • Plant and sustain

    Seed the clean ground and keep it alive with watering robots and refill tanks. Hexes grow from Clean to Planted to Thriving.

  • Ruins become cities

    Raise each base through five levels, from a scrapyard shack to a bright futuristic town with murals, lights and towers.

  • Fly rockets

    Rebuild the launch pads and fly between bases on sleek Mk I to IV rockets, with full launch and landing cinematics.

  • Photo mode

    Pause anywhere, pose Lumi, add a filter and capture the world you brought back. Wildlife visits make great portraits.

  • Made for Steam Deck

    Short satisfying loops, offline progress and scalable graphics presets make it a great couch and handheld game.
