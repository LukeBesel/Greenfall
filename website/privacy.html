Privacy

Privacy note

Short version: this site doesn't track you. The only personal data we collect is the email address you give us for the newsletter.

No tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ad or tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site itself. Like any web server, the host may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure.

The newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is sent to our newsletter provider, who stores it and sends the emails on our behalf. We use it only to send Greenfall news (launch dates, demo, occasional development notes). We never sell or share it.

  • You'll usually get a confirmation email first; nothing is sent until you confirm.
  • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes you from the list.
  • You can ask us at any time to see or delete what we hold about you: press@greenfall.example.

The provider may record whether emails were delivered and opened, as part of sending them. Its own privacy policy applies to that.

Things that only load when you press them

Some features are provided by other services and only contact them when you choose to use them: the trailer (YouTube, in privacy-enhanced mode, loaded only after you press play), store links (Steam), the checkout (once direct sales are open, our payment provider's checkout script loads on the home page so the Buy button can open it), and links to social networks. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this note changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
